# Privacy principles

Umliva's core checking is local-first and works offline after installation. Documents, OCR/PDF text, addresses, names, financial amounts, meter values, allocation values, reports and case content are not sent to Firebase or a required backend. Optional services are opt-in, and feedback previews the exact technical payload before transmission.

The public site intentionally contains no private implementation, test corpora, real documents, credentials or signing material. Umliva is a deterministic billing checker, not individualized legal advice.
